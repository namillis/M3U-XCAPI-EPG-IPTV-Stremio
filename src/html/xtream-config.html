Good to know

  • Big panels take 10-30 seconds to check. The first load in Stremio after a deploy or a long break can take a bit longer.
  • Channels and the guide refresh on their own. You only need to reinstall when you change a setting here.
  • Most accounts allow 1-2 streams at once. If a channel won't open, close the previous one and try again. "Check account" shows how many are in use.
