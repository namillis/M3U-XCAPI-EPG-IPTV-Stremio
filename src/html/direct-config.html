Good to know

  • Movies and series are detected from names and groups (e.g. S01E02 or a year in brackets), so results depend on how the playlist is labelled.
  • Channels and the guide refresh on their own. You only need to reinstall when you change a setting here.
  • If the playlist won't load here but works in other apps, your provider may block cloud servers.
