What you get

Now and next

Channel pages show the current show and what's coming up, in your timezone, with daylight saving handled.

Group rows

Each channel group, like USA | SPORTS, can be its own row on the Stremio home screen, filtered by keyword.

Live TV only

Drop movies and series completely for faster loads and lower memory.

Catch-up

On channels your Xtream panel records, start the current show over or watch shows from the last few days.

Configure from Stremio

The addon's Configure button reopens this site with your current settings filled in.

Encrypted install links

When the server has CONFIG_SECRET set, your login is encrypted inside the addon URL.

Stays up to date

Channels and guide data refresh on their own about every hour. No reinstall needed.

Light on memory

Big guides are streamed and trimmed to the next 36 hours, so it fits a free 512 MB host.

Tips

  • Changing a setting: open the addon in Stremio and click Configure, or set it up again here. Either way, reinstall with the new link.
  • Guide a few hours off: turn on "Guide times are already in my timezone" and leave EPG Offset at 0.
  • Too many rows: with group catalogs on, use "Only groups containing" to keep just the groups you watch.
  • A channel won't play: most accounts allow 1-2 streams at once. "Check account" on the Xtream page shows how many are in use.
